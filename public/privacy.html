Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Cyberpunk TCG Vault is an unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for tracking Cyberpunk TCG cards. This page explains what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in (email link or Google), we receive your email address and a user ID from our authentication provider, Supabase. With Google sign-in we request only your basic profile (name, email). We never see your Google password.
  • Your collection and decks: the cards, quantities, notes and decks you save are stored in our Supabase database and linked to your user ID.
  • Card scans: uploaded photos and cropped still frames taken with the live camera are sent to an AI vision model through OpenRouter to identify cards. The live preview stays on your device. We do not store your photos or frames. We keep a daily count of scans per user to enforce usage limits.
  • Local mode: if you don't sign in, your collection stays in your browser's local storage and never reaches our servers.
  • Web analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits, see which screens people use, and understand where visitors come from. Page-view URLs exclude query parameters and fragments. We do not attach your account, collection, or deck data to analytics.

What we don't do

We don’t run ads or sell your personal data.

Service providers

Supabase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting and web analytics), OpenRouter and the vision model provider it routes to (photo recognition only). Card images load from the official Cyberpunk TCG card database.

Security

Database row-level security means each account can only read and write its own collection and decks.

Deleting your data

You can clear your collection and delete decks in the app at any time. To delete your account entirely, open an issue (below) or contact the project owner, and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/LeviJohnsonNC/cyberpunk-tcg-vault/issues.
